## 📁 File Types Explained

### multi-entity-service.json
Contains company structures including:
- Parent companies (main entertainment entities)
- Subsidiary companies (record labels, fashion brands, real estate holdings)
- Tax IDs, currencies, and fiscal year information

**Status**: ✅ Created for all 12 countries

### product-service.json
Lists products and services offered:
- Music albums and singles
- Concert performances
- Brand endorsements
- Real estate rentals/sales
- Fashion products
- Media services

**Status**: ✅ Created for South Africa, Nigeria | ⏳ Pending for other countries

### customer-service.json
Business customers including:
- TV and radio stations
- Music streaming platforms
- Telecom companies
- Brand partnerships
- Event organizers
- Real estate clients

**Status**: ✅ Created for South Africa, Nigeria | ⏳ Pending for other countries

### vendor-service.json
Suppliers and service providers:
- Music equipment suppliers
- Production studios
- Event security
- Marketing agencies
- Legal services
- Property management

**Status**: ⏳ Pending for all countries

### current-user-context.json
User authentication and context:
- User information
- Company access
- Permissions
- Current session data

**Status**: ⏳ Pending for most countries
